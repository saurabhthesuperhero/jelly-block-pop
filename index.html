Jelly Block Pop

Drag jelly blocks, pop lines, chase combos.

A juicy block puzzle: drag colourful jelly blocks onto the 8×8 board and fill full rows or columns to pop them. Clear several lines at once for big combos and keep clearing for a streak bonus.

  • Classic: endless block puzzle, beat your best score and climb the leaderboard
  • Adventure: 300 levels, pop the lines that hold the gems
  • Fair pieces: there is always a way to keep going
  • Undo and New Pieces boosters, one continue per game
  • Plays offline, no timer
