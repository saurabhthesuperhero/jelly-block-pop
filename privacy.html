Privacy Policy – Jelly Block Pop

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Jelly Block Pop ("the game", package com.saikaizen.blockpuzzle) is a single-player mobile game published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any account. Your game progress (best score, saved game, adventure level, settings) is stored only on your device.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous gameplay events such as games started and finished, levels completed, boosters used, ads watched and purchases made, together with device information (model, Android version, language, country), app instance and advertising identifiers, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is not linked to your name and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads — see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the game asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it at any time from Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your best Classic score is sent to the Google Play Games leaderboard, where it is shown with your Play Games profile name and avatar. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Play Games app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details. Purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are checked on your device so that they can be restored.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a local reminder on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress lives only on your device: uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Data collected by Google for analytics and advertising is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

Security

Data exchanged with the analytics, advertising, Play Games and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
